than doing anything specific to the minute -- compare it here, not just random_direction.

'probe moved%'/'probe shift' (internal) verify the intervention internally: they measure
whether the probe's OWN readout of the angle moved toward the target, independent of
whether the stated answer did. If probe shift tracks alpha closely but the output columns
stay flat, steering is moving the representation but the output ignores it -- not that
steering failed. 'pert/resid' is the mean ratio of the perturbation's norm to each
image-token position's OWN residual-stream norm (report req: confirm steering isn't just
wrecking activations) -- by construction this tracks alpha closely; see
pert_rel_norm_min/max in the trials CSV for how much it varies position-to-position.
